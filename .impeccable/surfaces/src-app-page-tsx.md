# Homepage hero extension

Scope: replace only the first homepage section. Retain all lower sections and the existing brand/navigation. Persuade mode: show the user's actual visual work and provide access to each business page. Local preview only; no deployment.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the five supplied panoramic banners are the experience, displayed in filename order without redundant overlay copy.

OWN-WORLD: inherit the faceted frog, black/white/cyan palette and Archivo/Noto Sans SC typography. Use the original frog bitmap rather than inventing a new logo.

STORY: a brief frog reveal introduces the brand, then yields to the banners. Visitors browse automatically or select a business direction using compact controls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: fixed navigation above an unframed full-width 1920:520 banner. All embedded text and badges remain visible. Previous and next buttons sit at the image edges; slide indicators and pause are lightly overlaid at the bottom, without a separate black control bar. The company overview follows immediately.

FORM: direct user-pinned local extension, no seed required. Crossfade every five seconds. Pause on hover/focus, offscreen or hidden tab; reduced motion disables autoplay until requested. Intro uses a 4.4-second cyan, coral and acid spectrum-facet sequence, once per session, can be skipped, and never locks page scrolling.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
